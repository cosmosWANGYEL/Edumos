About Edumos

Bridging education beyond internet barriers — built for Bhutan, built for the future.

Our Story

Edumos was born out of a simple but powerful observation — thousands of students across Bhutan's remote Dzongkhags lose valuable learning time every day because of unstable or nonexistent internet connections.

Our team of students from the College of Science and Technology, Phuentsholing, set out to build a platform that works with the reality of connectivity in Bhutan, not against it. Edumos is an offline-first learning platform that lets teachers download content when online and share it locally with students — no internet required.

We believe every child in every corner of Bhutan deserves equal access to quality education, regardless of where they live or how strong their signal is.
