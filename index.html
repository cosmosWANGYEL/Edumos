What We Offer

👁

Vision

We envision a Bhutan where geography and connectivity are never barriers to learning. Every student — from Thimphu to the most remote village in Zhemgang — deserves access to quality education, and Edumos exists to make that a reality.

🎯

Mission

Our mission is to empower teachers and students across all 20 Dzongkhags with tools that work offline, sync automatically when online, and adapt to the real-world challenges of education in Bhutan. We build technology that serves people, not the other way around.

⚡

Features

Fully offline learning system — works without any internet connection
Teacher-to-student local sharing over Wi-Fi hotspot
PIN-protected subject modules for secure access
Quizzes, progress tracking, and performance reports
Automatic sync when internet is restored
💡

Why It Matters

Bhutan's mountainous terrain means that many schools operate in areas where internet access is unreliable or simply unavailable. Students in these areas are falling behind not because of lack of effort, but because of lack of access. Edumos directly solves this problem by bringing education to the device, not depending on the cloud.
